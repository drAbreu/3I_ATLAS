# Plot 4C: Deceleration G-Forces

This plot calculates the mechanical stress (G-forces) acting on a spacecraft trying to stop at Jupiter using its atmosphere.

## What the Plot Shows
The plot estimates the **Peak Deceleration** in "G's" (multiples of Earth's gravity) for a range of atmospheric entry altitudes.
- **Galileo Probe Reference:** Experienced ~230 G's.
- **3I/ATLAS Scenario:** Estimates show deceleration peaks exceeding **500-1000 G's** for high-speed interstellar capture.

## Key Takeaway
Even if a material can survive the *heat* (Plot 4A), the *mechanical stress* of slowing down from 80+ km/s at Jupiter would crush almost any current human spacecraft. A 3I/ATLAS-like capture implies a vehicle built with materials and structures far more robust than anything we've sent to space.
