# Plot 2A: Reproduction of Hibberd et al. (2025)

This plot reproduces Figure 4 from the paper "Is the interstellar object 3I/ATLAS alien technology?" to verify the initial "nudge" claim.

## The Science
Based on the **initial orbital elements** published in mid-2025 ($a=-0.2638, e=6.1396$), the paper calculated that a maneuver performed in mid-2025 could intercept 3I/ATLAS at Jupiter with a $\Delta V$ of only **~5 km/s**.

## What the Plot Shows
The curve demonstrates a clear "valley" in early 2025 where the intercept cost is at its minimum. This $\Delta V$ is well within the capability of current chemical rockets like the Falcon Heavy or SLS.

## Key Takeaway
We successfully reproduced the paper's findings, confirming that *with the initial data*, an intercept mission appeared highly feasible with existing technology.
