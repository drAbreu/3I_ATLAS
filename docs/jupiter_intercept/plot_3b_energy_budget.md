# Plot 3B: Energy Budget ($C_3$)

This plot tracks the **Characteristic Energy ($C_3$)** required for a mission as a function of the maneuver date. It provides a clearer look at how the energy "cost" evolves as 3I/ATLAS approaches its perijove.

## What the Plot Shows
$C_3$ (square of the hyperbolic excess velocity) is a standard measure of launch energy.
- **Intercept Scenario (Blue):** Shows the $C_3$ required to simply hit Jupiter.
- **Rendezvous Scenario (Orange):** Shows the $C_3$ required to stop at Jupiter.

The vertical dashed line marks the **Discovery Date (July 1, 2025)**.

## Key Takeaway
The plot shows that the energy "window" is relatively flat for the intercept scenario but climbs sharply for the rendezvous scenario as the object gets closer to Jupiter. This indicates that any mission to actually orbit Jupiter alongside 3I/ATLAS would have needed to be launched almost immediately upon discovery to take advantage of the minimum energy window.
