# Plot 1C: Distribution of Close Approaches

This analysis isolates the population of interstellar visitors that pass specifically through Jupiter's neighborhood to understand the relative frequency of "deep" encounters.

## Methodology
Using a sample of **10,000 trajectories** that pass within 100 million km of Jupiter (derived from a larger 400,000-trial simulation), we analyze how these trajectories are distributed across different proximity levels.

## What the Plot Shows
The histogram shows the number of cases at each distance from Jupiter. 
- The **x-axis** is the closest approach distance in million km (log scale).
- The **y-axis** is the count of simulations.

It highlights that while many objects pass in the "outer" neighborhood, a significant number still reach the inner complex of the Hill sphere and Lagrange points.

## Key Takeaway
When we focus on the population of objects that *already* pass near Jupiter's orbit, a pass within the Hill radius (or near the L1/L2 points) is not a rare occurrence—it represents a large subset of all near-Jupiter visitors.
